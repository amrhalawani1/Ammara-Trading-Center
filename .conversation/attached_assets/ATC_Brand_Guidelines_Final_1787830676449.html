01 — Brand Foundation

Who We Are

Before any logo, color, or font — this is the foundation. Every design decision, every caption, every photo should reflect these truths about Amara Trading Center.

Our Story

Amara Trading Center has been serving Amman since 1977. What started as a family business has grown into Jordan's trusted destination for premium kitchen accessories, built-in appliances, and hardware. Nearly five decades of expertise — and we still treat every client like our first.

Our Mission

To bring world-class kitchen solutions to Jordanian homes and projects. We believe the kitchen is the heart of the home — and it deserves products built to last, designed with precision, and installed with care.

Our Vision

To be the most trusted name in premium kitchen solutions in Jordan — where designers, contractors, and homeowners come first when quality is non-negotiable.

Our Values

Quality without compromise. We carry only what we believe in.

Family integrity. 47 years built on trust, not transactions.

Expert guidance. We don't just sell — we advise.

06 — Photography

Visual Language

ATC photography should feel like a luxury design magazine — dark, considered, product-first. Every image should make the viewer want to touch the product.

01

Dark backgrounds preferred

Black or very dark grey makes products look premium. Avoid busy backgrounds or cluttered surfaces that compete with the product.

02

Natural or controlled lighting only

Harsh flash or yellow overhead light makes products look cheap. Natural window light or a diffused ring light only. Never direct flash.

03

Product fills 60–70% of frame

Get close. Let the product dominate. Slightly off-center compositions feel more editorial and intentional than dead-center shots.

04

One Lightroom preset — always

Slightly warm shadows, lifted blacks, slightly desaturated. Create once in Lightroom Mobile. Apply to every photo before posting. Consistency is the goal.

05

Show function, not just form

A drawer opening. Water into the sink. A door closing softly. Movement makes products feel real and desirable — not just decorative objects.

06

Always shoot vertical for social

Instagram is 9:16 for Stories/Reels and 4:5 for feed. Always shoot vertically or leave room to crop without losing the product.

08 — Brand Misuse

What to Avoid

These are the most common mistakes that damage the ATC brand. Anyone creating content for Amara Trading Center — internally or externally — must follow these without exception.

❌ Cheap graphics

No clip art, watermarked stock photos, or generic rainbow Canva templates. Every graphic must feel premium or it doesn't go out.

❌ Informal writing

No excessive emojis, ALL CAPS shouting, or "limited time offer!!!" language. ATC speaks with confidence, not desperation.

❌ Wrong colors

No purple, blue, green, or anything outside the ATC palette — even for one post. Color consistency is what builds recognition.

❌ Low-quality photos

Blurry, yellow-lit, or cluttered photos never go out. If the shot isn't good enough — reshoot it. Quality always over speed.

❌ Multiple CTAs

Never end a post with multiple actions. One CTA per post only. Make the decision easy for the customer — don't overwhelm them.

❌ Wrong fonts

No Comic Sans, Impact, or random downloads. Cormorant Garamond for headlines. Montserrat for body. Nothing else, ever.

❌ No approval flow

Linah prepares content. Ahmad approves. No exceptions for campaigns, pricing posts, or partnership announcements.

❌ Inconsistent posting

10 posts one week, silence for 3 weeks destroys trust and kills reach. 4 posts every single week — no gaps, no exceptions.

❌ Altering the logo

Never change colors, remove the italic, stretch it, or add effects to the logo. The ///, the A, and MARA are one locked, untouchable unit.
